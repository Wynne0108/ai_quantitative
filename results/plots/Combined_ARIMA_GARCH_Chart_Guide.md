### 6. Risk Metrics (VaR) | 風險指標 (VaR)
**Location | 位置**: Bottom right | 右下角  
**Colors | 顏色**: Light blue, Light coral, Light green, Light yellow | 淺藍色，淺珊瑚色，淺綠色，淺黃色

**What it shows | 顯示內容**:
- **1D 95%**: 1-day Value at Risk at 95% confidence | 95% 信心水準的 1 日風險價值
- **1D 99%**: 1-day Value at Risk at 99% confidence | 99% 信心水準的 1 日風險價值  
- **7D 95%**: 7-day Value at Risk at 95% confidence | 95% 信心水準的 7 日風險價值
- **7D 99%**: 7-day Value at Risk at 99% confidence | 99% 信心水準的 7 日風險價值

**Understanding VaR | 理解 VaR**:
VaR answers: "What is the maximum loss I could face with X% confidence over Y days?"
VaR 回答："在 Y 天內以 X% 的信心，我可能面臨的最大損失是多少？"

**Practical interpretation | 實際解釋**:
```
1D 95% VaR = $1,500 means:
1 日 95% VaR = $1,500 意味著：
"95% of the time, daily loss won't exceed $1,500"
"95% 的時間，日損失不會超過 $1,500"

7D 95% VaR = $4,000 means:  
7 日 95% VaR = $4,000 意味著：
"95% of the time, weekly loss won't exceed $4,000"
"95% 的時間，週損失不會超過 $4,000"
```

**Position sizing guidance | 倉位規模指導**:
```
If your risk tolerance is $2,000:
如果你的風險承受能力是 $2,000：

1D 95% VaR = $1,500 → Safe position size
1 日 95% VaR = $1,500 → 安全倉位大小

1D 95% VaR = $3,000 → Reduce position by 50%  
1 日 95% VaR = $3,000 → 減少 50% 倉位

7D 95% VaR = $6,000 → Consider hedging for weekly exposure
7 日 95% VaR = $6,000 → 考慮對週度風險敞口進行對沖
```

## 🎯 Integrated Analysis Workflow | 綜合分析工作流程

### Step 1: Price Direction Analysis | 步驟 1：價格方向分析
1. Check **ARIMA forecast** for price trend | 檢查 **ARIMA 預測** 的價格趨勢
2. Assess **confidence interval** width | 評估 **信心區間** 寬度
3. Confirm with **trading signals** | 用 **交易信號** 確認

### Step 2: Risk Assessment | 步驟 2：風險評估  
1. Review **volatility forecast** for uncertainty | 查看 **波動率預測** 的不確定性
2. Check **VaR metrics** for position sizing | 檢查 **VaR 指標** 進行倉位配置
3. Analyze **returns distribution** for tail risks | 分析 **收益率分佈** 的尾部風險

### Step 3: Model Validation | 步驟 3：模型驗證
1. Examine **ARIMA residuals** for model adequacy | 檢查 **ARIMA 殘差** 的模型充分性
2. Look for systematic patterns or biases | 尋找系統性模式或偏差
3. Consider model limitations in decision making | 在決策中考慮模型限制

❌ VaR 水平較高

## 📚 Advanced Tips | 進階技巧

### Chart Correlation Analysis | 圖表關聯分析

**Price vs Volatility | 價格 vs 波動率**:
- High volatility often precedes trend changes | 高波動率通常預示趨勢變化
- Low volatility may indicate trend continuation | 低波動率可能表示趨勢延續

**Signals vs Residuals | 信號 vs 殘差**:
- Large residuals near signal changes may indicate false signals | 信號變化附近的大殘差可能表示假信號
- Consistent small residuals support signal reliability | 持續的小殘差支持信號可靠性

### Time Horizon Considerations | 時間範圍考慮

**Short-term (1-3 days) | 短期 (1-3 天)**:
- Focus on 1D VaR and immediate volatility forecasts | 關注 1 日 VaR 和即時波動率預測
- Trading signals are most reliable | 交易信號最可靠

**Medium-term (1-4 weeks) | 中期 (1-4 週)**:  
- Use 7D VaR for position sizing | 使用 7 日 VaR 進行倉位配置
- ARIMA forecasts have moderate reliability | ARIMA 預測具有中等可靠性

**Long-term (1+ months) | 長期 (1+ 月)**:
- Focus on returns distribution for tail risk assessment | 關注收益率分佈進行尾部風險評估
- All forecasts should be taken as directional guidance only | 所有預測僅作為方向性指導

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimers | 重要免責聲明

**Model Limitations | 模型限制**:
- ARIMA assumes linear relationships | ARIMA 假設線性關係
- GARCH may not capture all volatility patterns | GARCH 可能無法捕獲所有波動模式  
- Historical patterns may not predict future performance | 歷史模式可能無法預測未來表現

**Risk Management | 風險管理**:
- Never rely solely on model predictions | 絕不僅依賴模型預測
- Always use proper position sizing | 始終使用適當的倉位配置
- Consider fundamental analysis alongside technical models | 在技術模型之外考慮基本面分析
- Market conditions can change rapidly | 市場條件可能迅速變化

---

💡 **Remember | 記住**: These models are tools to enhance your analysis, not replace human judgment. Always combine quantitative insights with market knowledge and proper risk management.
